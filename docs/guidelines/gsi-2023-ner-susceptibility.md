The Geological Survey of India classifies Aizawl Ridge, Churachandpur Basin and Gangtok Valley as high susceptibility due to shale overburden, 25–40° slopes, and >1200 mm seasonal rainfall. Mitigation: benching, shotcrete, and drainage. Monitoring requires hourly rainfall + pore pressure. Districts Mizoram, Manipur and Sikkim share this lithology; field verification every quarter updates the susceptibility layer used by M1.
