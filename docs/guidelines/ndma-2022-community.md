title: "NDMA Landslide Do/Don't — Community"
source: NDMA
year: 2022
---

Do: evacuate on warning, avoid cut slopes after 50 mm/24h. Don't: block drainage, build on toe of slope. Early warning thresholds: 75 mm/3d antecedent + 20 mm/12h forecast triggers Watch; 120 mm/3d + 40 mm/12h triggers Warning. These thresholds are the basis for GeoSentinel alert rules — Watch, Warning, Evacuation severity maps to NDMA community SOP.
